Our Mission

Equipping youth with "++factors" for tomorrow's world.

The task for marginalized communities in developing countries is all the more daunting to produce a generation of youth equipped with 21st Century skills or "++factors" — ability to think "outside of the box", problem-solving, communication skills, and an ability to work both as part of a team and independently, and across different industries.

TAUS Foundation non-profit and 501(c)(3) organization, has launched a multi-pronged, multi-phase initiative to make available 21st century skills to the underprivileged, marginalized children and youth of our country.

Our Three Pillars

Building the next generation of innovators

A holistic framework that grows minds, sharpens character, and opens the door to modern technology.

Academic Growth

Hands-on experience while developing STEM skills that will enhance and boost the academic growth.

Personality Development

Inculcate confidence, foster critical thinking, collaboration, and persistence besides developing problem solving and design thinking.

Advance Technology

With access to modern technology students get to write code, use algorithms, and develop robots using advance engineering.

Our Programs

Let's Help Students Create their Future

NGSS CCSS CSTA

TAUS is creating opportunities for the students to carve out their future. It is providing them a platform to hone modern 21st Century skills. All the projects we teach the students are based on internationally recognized STEM frameworks and standards like NGSS (Next Generation Science Standards) or CCSS (Common Core State Standards) or CSTA (Computer Science Teachers Association).
