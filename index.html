Our Signature

Honey Comb

خلية النحل · Khaliat Al-Nahl

A beloved Yemeni dessert — pillowy milk-bread pulled into a honeycomb, baked golden, finished with toasted sesame, and filled with soft cheese before a generous drizzle of honey.

Warm, gooey, and just sweet enough — it's the one people come back for. Best enjoyed fresh with a cup of Irani chai or our premium organic coffee.

Our Story

Where chai, coffee & fresh bakery meet

Under the glow of our Roman-numeral clock and hand-painted Turkish lamps, Qahwa House brings together two great traditions: the unhurried ritual of Irani chai and the rich aroma of premium organic coffee.

Alongside, our bakers fire the ovens from morning — cakes, Osmania biscuits, buns, and the Yemeni honey comb people travel for. Pull up a chair across our two floors, with a dedicated family section upstairs.
