Honey Comb

خلية النحل · Khaliat Al-Nahl

A beloved Yemeni dessert — pillowy milk-bread pulled into a honeycomb, baked golden, finished with toasted sesame, and filled with soft cheese before a generous drizzle of honey.

Warm, gooey, and just sweet enough — it's the one people come back for. Best enjoyed fresh with a cup of Irani chai or our premium organic coffee.
