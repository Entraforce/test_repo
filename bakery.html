Our Signature

Honey Comb

خلية النحل · Khaliat Al-Nahl

A beloved Yemeni dessert — pillowy milk-bread pulled into a honeycomb, baked golden, finished with toasted sesame, and filled with soft cheese before a generous drizzle of honey.

Warm, gooey, and just sweet enough — it's the one people come back for. Best enjoyed fresh with a cup of Irani chai or our premium organic coffee.

What We Bake

Fresh from morning till midnight

From dawn, the counter fills with soft buns, maska and malai buns, flaky chicken and veg puffs, and trays of golden Osmania, coconut, fruit and chocolate-cashew biscuits — the classic companions to a cup of Irani chai.

Through the day come the cakes and pastries: red velvet, choco truffle, black forest, Biscoff cheesecake, dark chocolate and fruit pastries, puddings and our apricot delight. And always, the Yemeni Honey Comb — warm, gooey and unmissable.
